# CLIENT BRIEF

Brand: Himalayan Harvest

Initial product: A new interpretation of chili oil infused with distinctive Himalayan spices.

Brand context: Himalayan Harvest is being developed as a broader parent brand, with this chili oil as one of its first products. Over time, the company intends to expand into a wider collection of organic food products inspired by the ingredients and flavors of the Himalayan region.

Origin: Sikkim, India.

The regional origin is an important part of the brand story. The company wants to support the local economy by encouraging organic farming and helping locally produced ingredients reach a wider international audience.

Purpose: Strengthen the local agricultural economy while creating greater recognition and demand for produce from the region.

Mission: Introduce people around the world to distinctive flavors and ingredients from the Himalayas.

Long-term vision: Build a food brand whose Himalayan ingredients and flavors can become familiar staples in kitchens internationally.

Target audience: Modern, conscious consumers who are curious about food, enjoy discovering and experimenting with new flavors, and care about quality, authenticity, origin, craft, and the story behind what they purchase.

Core values:

Ethical farming.
Sustainability.
Authenticity.
Regional pride.
Quality.
Respect for origin.

The chili oil should feel versatile enough to naturally belong in many different eating occasions rather than being restricted to one cuisine or dish.

The identity must work strongly for the launch product while being flexible enough to become the visual foundation for the entire Himalayan Harvest parent brand.
